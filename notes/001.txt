Title: I believe in you :)

Subtitle: "Whether you think you can, or you think you can't, you're right" - Henry Ford

Description
Most people are capable of so much more than they think.
You are too.

Some people are lucky - they were believed in by someone, or many people, from a young age.
A parent, a teacher, a coach - it doesn't matter - they had someone to say "You've got this!" when they fell over.

Over time, they got so used to falling, dusting themselves off, and trying again that the message changed:
"You've got this!" became: **I've got this**.

They repeated this process so much that they began to achieve awesome things. And soon, even more people believed in them!

With all this external belief; all the trying and failing, and then failing and succeeding, the most important thing happened...
They developed that self-sustaining jet fuel behind every great accomplishment... **self-belief**.

Then, fueled by self-belief, they stopped needing someone else to believe in them. It also didn't matter if people **didn't believe in them**. They would just prove the doubters wrong :)

However, the trick is having **someone believe in you initially**. 
The most successful people in the world aren't special. These people are *YOU*. They just had a helping hand.

That's why I believe in you.
You - yes, you - have got this. I'll say it again so that doubting voice in your head can hush. You. Have. Got. This!

It might take a while - perhaps a long while - but you will start believing in yourself. And you'll be amazed at what you achieve and the person you will become.

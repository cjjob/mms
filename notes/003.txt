Title: Just don't quit


Subtitle: The only guaranteed way to fail is to quit.


Description
Do anything other than quit.

Changing direction isn't quitting. Sometimes a thing just isn't for you. Stop, reorient, go again. That's normal. That's progress.

But *never quit on yourself*.

And be careful. Reorienting all the time can be quitting in disguise. If the problem is always out there, look inside.

So, you're on the right path. You know it is. But it's hard.

DON'T QUIT. Instead:
- Complain
- Eat ice cream
- Go for a walk. In nature, even better.
- Nap
- Talk to a friend
- Binge your favourite show
- Take a day off. Or a week.

Then go again. You didn't quit. You took a break. We all do.

The difference between people who do awesome things and people who don't? One group just doesn't quit.

You've got this.

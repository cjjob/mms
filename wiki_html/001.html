I believe in you :)

"Whether you think you can, or you think you can't, you're right" - Henry Ford

Most people are capable of so much more than they think. You are too.

Some people are lucky - they were believed in by someone, or many people, from a young age. A parent, teacher, a sports coach - it doesn't matter - they had someone to say "You've got this!" when they fell over.

The magic is that, over time, they got so used to falling over, dusting themselves off, and going again that the message was internalised. "You've got this!" became: I've got this.

They repeated this process so much that they started achieving awesome things. Soon, even more people believed in them!

With all this external belief; all the trying and failing, and then failing and succeeding, the most important thing happened... They developed that self-sustaining jet fuel behind every great accomplishment... self-belief.

Then, fueled by self-belief, they stopped needing someone else to believe in them, and it also stopped mattering if anyone didn't believe in them. They would just prove the doubters wrong :)

However, the trick to bootstraping this entire process is having someone believe in you initially. It's the catalyst for awesomeness! The most successful people in the world aren't special. These people are YOU. They just had a helping hand.

That's why I believe in you. You - yes, you - have got this. I'll say it again so that doubting voice in your head can hush. You. Have. Got. This!

It might take a while - perhaps a long while - but you will start believing in yourself. And when you do that, you'll be amazed at what you achieve and the person you will become.

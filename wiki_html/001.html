I believe in you :)

"Whether you think you can, or you think you can't, you're right" - Henry Ford

Most people are capable of so much more than they think. You are too. Some people are lucky - they are believed in by someone, or many people from a young age. Parents, teachers, a sports coach, etc. just someone to say "You've got this!" when they fall over, or miss their target. The magic is that, over time, they get so used to falling over, dusting themselves off, and going again that the message is internalised. "You've got this!" becomes _I've_ got this. They do this so much that they start achieving awesome things, and now even more people believe in them. Why not, right? At this point all the data says that complete the things they start, get the highest scores, win the matches, run the fastest, and so on. Concurrently, the most important thing happens... Self-belief is born! They've done so much - achieved so much - that they _know_ they can do more, perhaps close to anything. And, critically, it doesn't even matter if anyone else believes in them anymore. They got that self-sustaining jet fuel behind every great accomplishment; self-belief.

But, the trick to bootstrap this entire process is having someone believe in you. It's the catalyst from awesomeness! These people aren't special. These people are YOU. That's why I believe in you. You - yes, you - have got this this. I'll say it again so that doubting voice in your head can hush. You've got this. So, go again champ. It might take a while - perhaps a long while - but you'll start believing in yourself. And when that happens I can't wait to see the things you'll do and the person you'll become.

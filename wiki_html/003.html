Just don't quit

The only way to guarantee failure is to quit.

Do anything other than quit.

You don't have to do everything to it's end. Sometimes things simply aren't for you. That's not quitting. If you stop, reorient, and continue in a new direction... that's expected. It's normal. It's progress.

But do not quit on yourself.

Also beware or reorienting too often. This could be quitting in disguise. If everything outside is always wrong, you need to look inside.

Now, let's say you're on the path. You're sure it's right. But it's hard. DO NOT QUIT. Do one of the following: - Complain - Eat ice cream - Go for walk - in nature = better - Nap - Meditate - Talk to friends - Take a day, or longer, off - Binge watch your favourite TV series - etc.

Then continue. You didn't quit. You had a break. We all do. Go again. You got this.

The biggest difference between people that do awesome things and people that don't is that one group just does not quit.

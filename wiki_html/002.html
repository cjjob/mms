Be 100% you

You have something special to offer.

Being 100% you is perhaps the single most important, and single most difficult task you will undertake.

It is important because once you do it, you will find your place in the world, achieve everything that you are capable of, and find an inner peace and self-acceptance that will carry you through any challenge life throws your way.

It is difficult because being 100% you first requires knowing who you really are, and who you are not.
You will need to cut out the noise of who your parents want you to be, what your current environment pushes you towards, what society tells you are, and what social media pressures you into
It requires understanding what you value, what you think matters, and what impact you want to have in your time on this earth.

Difficult? Yes.
Confusing? Yes.
Full of failure? Of course - but so is every great success.

How to do this is tricky - I will give some tips below. The primary message here is that you must do it.
For once you are 100% you, you will enrich the world, and have your own life enriched in a way that is incomparable with any other mode of existing.

Put differently - and forgive the morbid language - if never become your absolute, authentic, brilliant self, you are doomed to walking in your own shadow, living a fractional life of the life you ought to live.

Tips for being 100% you:
1. Find and move to the spaces that accept you as you would like to be. (This is far easier written than done - but good luck!)
2. Accept the discomfort of change. To become who you are supposed to be you will have to stop being, in some ways at least, who you are now. This won't feel right until it's complete.
3. Be brave. You will have to try things. Fail at things. Disappoint people. Disappoint yourself. Circle back. Question deeply held ideas of yourself and the world. The list is long, but bravery will see you last the course.
4. Be kind. Be kind to yourself. If you aren't kind to yourself the process will be too painful, too arduous and you will stop short of who you could be.

Good luck. You can do this. You're going to be something great.

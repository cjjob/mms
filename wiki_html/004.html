Winners fail (most)

To win you must try. To try you will fail. To win you must/will fail.

Not only do winners fail. Winners fail more than anybody.

This isn't complicated. Not matter how talented you are, or special you are, at some point you will reach a point where you can't do something. Maybe you fail once. Maybe twice. It takes a real winner to fail 10,000 times.

Failing and progress are largely the same thing. The difference is your perspective.

'Loser': "I failed 20 times." 'Winner': "I'm 20 tries closer to success."

'Loser': "Each of my 7 ideas were wrong." 'Winner': "I've eliminated 7 candidate.

'Loser': 'This is hopeless. I'm a failure. I'll never do this.' 'Winner': 'I've been here before. I succeeded before. I'm sure I'll do this.'

You can choose either. Who do you want to be?

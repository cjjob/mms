Simple, clean hand-drawn line art on a pure white background, loosely in the spirit of the
reference images (a general guide, not something to copy). Even,
confident dark outlines; stick-figure characters with round heads and simple, expressive faces;
props and places drawn with just enough lines to read at a glance. Colour is used sparingly, as
coloured lines and small accents such as the main character's blue scarf; everything else is line
work on white, with no shading, gradients or large filled areas. Friendly and uncluttered: few lines, each one doing work.

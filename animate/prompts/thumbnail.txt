WHAT TO DRAW: the episode's main theme as a single, bold visual metaphor, the one image that sums
up the title and the narration. The main character is in it, large, with a clear, expressive face
and pose. One idea only: the character plus one or two big, simple props, and almost nothing else.
No background details, no secondary characters unless the idea needs one. It must read instantly
at the size of a thumbnail: big shapes, thick confident lines, lots of white space around them.

SERIES BIBLE (the same for every episode)
Purpose: the words and pictures together are motivational and informative.
Tone: relaxed and fun, always wholesome. Now and then, not too often, a frame gets a comic touch,
such as an over-the-top depiction of the idea or a background detail reacting. Serious or
emotional beats stay sincere.
Main character, in every episode and drawn the same way every time: a simple stick figure with a
large round head, big round eyes, a small expressive mouth, a thin single-line body and
limbs, rounded hands and feet, and a blue scarf. Other characters are told apart from them by one
bold, simple feature (a hat, a ponytail, a different scarf colour, a taller build).
Visual storytelling: each frame expresses one central idea as a clear, simple visual metaphor,
with clean characters and a little background detail so it never feels empty. Each episode
invents its own scenes and metaphors from its narration.
Continuity: one consistent world per episode. Anything introduced appears in at least a few
frames unless there's a clear reason to drop it. Scene changes are welcome when they give the
story a sense of flow; make them readable, e.g. the character walks into the new place.
Always the same across episodes: the main character as described above, the line weight and line
style, and the pure white background.

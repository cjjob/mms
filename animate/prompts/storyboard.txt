YOUR TASK
The narration is below with every word numbered. Split it into frames and give each a scene and
a camera move. How many frames, where they cut and what they show is up to you: cut where the
picture should change, often right on a key word.

Write each "scene" for the drawing model: a short narrative description of one still moment,
covering who is in it (by their cast names), what they are doing and feeling, the props, and
where the main action sits in the frame. One moment per frame, never a sequence or split panels.

Return ONLY JSON, no commentary, in this shape:
{"cast": [{"name": "...", "look": "..."}, ...],
  "settings": [{"name": "...", "look": "...", "background": "..."}],
  "frames": [{"start": 0, "text": "...", "setting": "...", "cast": ["..."], "scene": "...",
              "continuity": "...",
              "camera": {"move": "zoom_in", "focus": {"x": 0.5, "y": 0.35}, "intensity": 0.5}}, ...]}

Fields:
- "cast": everyone who appears, each with a short "name" and a "look" precise enough to draw them
  identically every time. The first is the main character, with the look exactly as the series
  bible describes it; others are told apart by one bold, simple feature.
- "settings": the places the story happens. "look" is the fixed layout (ground line, main objects
  and where each sits); "background" is a few small details that stay in place in every frame there.
- "start": the number of the word where the frame begins. The first frame starts at 0 and starts
  strictly increase; a frame lasts until the next one starts, the last runs to the end ({last_word}).
- "text": the words the frame covers.
- "setting": this frame's setting, by its "name".
- "cast" (in a frame): the names of the characters visible in it, at most 3.
- "scene": what the frame shows, as described above. Keep signs, screens and objects wordless.
- "continuity": what changed since the previous frame that the drawing must show (e.g. "Sam now
  holds the map; the bird has flown to the fence"), or "" if nothing did.
- "camera": a slow move over the frame's duration, applied to the finished image.
  - "move": "hold", "zoom_in", "zoom_out" or "pan".
  - "focus": the point of interest as fractions of the frame (x 0 = left, 1 = right; y 0 = top,
    1 = bottom), inside the main band ({safe_top_pct} to {caption_top_pct} down). A zoom closes in on it or pulls back from it; a pan
    travels towards it. The drawing model is told this point, so it puts the key detail there.
  - "intensity": optional, 0-1 (default 0.5): how strong the move is.

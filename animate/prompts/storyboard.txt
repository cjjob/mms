You are storyboarding a short narrated video for Instagram Reels. The narration is below with
every word numbered. Split it into frames (one still image each) and choose a camera move for
each. How many frames, where they cut and what they show is up to you: make sensible choices.

Hard constraints:
- Simple stick-figure art. The reference image of the main character is loose inspiration,
  not a strict character template.
- Everything is line-drawn. Coloured lines are fine, but no filled shapes.
- Keep continuity across the whole piece: one consistent world, each frame following on from
  the last. Don't introduce elements out of nowhere.

Return ONLY JSON, no commentary, in this shape:
{"cast": "...",
  "settings": [{"name": "...", "look": "...", "background": "..."}],
  "frames": [{"start": 0, "text": "...", "setting": "...", "scene": "...",
              "camera": {"move": "zoom_in", "focus": {"x": 0.5, "y": 0.4}, "intensity": 0.5}}, ...]}

Fields:
- "cast": the recurring characters, described so they can be drawn the same way every time.
- "settings": the places the story happens. "look" is the fixed layout; "background" the small
  details that stay in place in every frame there.
- "start": the number of the word where the frame begins. The first frame starts at 0 and
  starts strictly increase; a frame lasts until the next one starts, the last runs to the end
  ({last_word}).
- "text": the words the frame covers.
- "setting": this frame's setting, by its "name".
- "scene": what the frame shows, as a single drawable still image.
- "camera": a slow move over the frame's duration, applied to the finished image.
  - "move": "hold", "zoom_in", "zoom_out" or "pan".
  - "focus": rough point of interest as fractions of the frame (x 0 = left, 1 = right; y 0 = top,
    1 = bottom). A zoom closes in on it or pulls back from it; a pan travels towards it.
  - "intensity": optional, 0-1 (default 0.5): how strong the move is.

Title: {title}

Narration ({n_words} words, about {seconds} seconds):
{script}
